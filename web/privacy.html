Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026 · South African privacy law applies
Privacy matters: Gearsh uses personal information to operate the marketplace, facilitate bookings and payments, communicate with users, protect the Platform and meet legal obligations. Gearsh does not sell personal information.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how Gearsh (Pty) Ltd processes personal information when you use Gearsh websites, applications, booking services and related Platform features.

2. Information we collect

  • Identity and contact information such as name, email address and phone number
  • Artist profile information such as skills, categories, location, pricing, portfolio and availability
  • Booking information such as service, date, venue/location, communications and booking history
  • Payment, payout and transaction information required to facilitate payments and settlements
  • Messages, reviews, support requests, dispute information and other information you submit
  • Device, browser, log and usage information needed to operate and secure the Platform

3. How we use personal information

  • Create and manage accounts and profiles
  • Enable discovery, booking, agreements, payments and payouts
  • Communicate booking, account, payment and service notifications
  • Verify information and reduce fraud, abuse and payment risk
  • Provide customer support and manage disputes
  • Improve reliability, security, performance and Platform functionality
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting and payment-provider obligations

4. Information shared to operate a booking

Gearsh may share information with the other party to a booking where reasonably necessary to arrange and perform the service. Payment and payout information may be shared with payment processors and financial service providers for transaction processing. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, court order, regulatory authority or legitimate security process.

5. Payment information

Gearsh uses third-party payment infrastructure for applicable transactions, including PayFast in South Africa. Payment providers may process payment information under their own terms, security controls and privacy policies. Gearsh receives and stores transaction information necessary to reconcile bookings, refunds, disputes and payouts.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Gearsh may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, preferences, security, analytics and Platform functionality. You can control cookies through your browser, although disabling required technologies may affect functionality.

7. Data security

Gearsh uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Retention

Gearsh retains personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account operation, bookings, financial reconciliation, dispute handling, fraud prevention and legal or regulatory recordkeeping.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to personal information held about you, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where permitted, restriction or objection to certain processing, and information about how your personal information is used. Requests can be made through the contact details below. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the South African Information Regulator.

10. Direct marketing and communications

Gearsh may send service-related communications necessary to operate your account or bookings. Where consent is required for marketing communications, Gearsh will use appropriate consent and opt-out mechanisms.

11. International processing

As Gearsh develops services for users beyond South Africa, information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure located outside South Africa. Where applicable, Gearsh will apply safeguards required by law for cross-border transfers.

12. Children

Gearsh is not intended to collect personal information from children in circumstances prohibited by applicable law. If you believe information has been collected improperly, contact us so that it can be reviewed.

13. Changes to this policy

Gearsh may update this policy when its services, legal obligations or data practices change. Material changes may be communicated through the Platform or another appropriate channel.

14. Contact

Privacy requests and questions can be sent to privacy@thegearsh.com.
