Booking Service Agreement

Last updated: 17 September 2026 · Applies to confirmed Gearsh bookings
How this works: This agreement governs the booking-specific relationship between a Client and an independent Artist. Gearsh provides the marketplace and transaction infrastructure; the Artist provides the underlying creative service.

1. Parties and booking

This Booking Service Agreement applies when a Client requests a service from an Artist through Gearsh and the booking is accepted and confirmed. The Client and Artist are the contracting parties for the underlying creative service. Gearsh facilitates the booking, agreement, payment collection, dispute process and payout infrastructure.

2. Service

The Artist agrees to provide the service described in the booking, including the agreed date, time, location, deliverables, duration, inclusions, exclusions and other requirements recorded through Gearsh. The Client agrees to provide accurate information and reasonable access or conditions required for delivery.

3. Price and Gearsh fees

The Artist service subtotal is the amount charged for the underlying service. Under the current Gearsh pricing model, Gearsh charges the Artist a 4% fee and the Client a 12.6% fee. The applicable Client total and Artist payout calculation are presented through the booking/payment process.

Gearsh currently absorbs applicable PayFast processing costs. No Artist deposit is required under the current V1 model.

4. Booking and payment

A booking request is not a confirmed engagement until the Artist accepts it and the Client completes any required payment. Once payment is successfully processed, the applicable funds may be held pending completion and the dispute period. Payment processing is provided through the payment infrastructure made available by Gearsh, including PayFast for applicable South African transactions.

5. Cancellation by Client

Unless the booking records a different lawful term agreed through Gearsh, the standard cancellation schedule applies to the Artist service subtotal:

Cancellation timingRefund
More than 7 days before booking100% of Artist subtotal
48 hours to 7 days before booking50% of Artist subtotal
Less than 48 hours before booking0% of Artist subtotal

Gearsh fees are retained on Client cancellations under the standard schedule. The Artist receives R0 when the Client cancels.

6. Cancellation or no-show by Artist

If the Artist cancels a confirmed booking or fails to attend or deliver the agreed service without an agreed or qualifying reason, the Client is entitled to a full refund of the amount paid, including applicable Gearsh fees, subject to payment-provider and legal requirements. The Artist receives R0 for the cancelled/no-show booking.

7. Client no-show

If the Client fails to attend or is unavailable after the Artist has attended or otherwise made the agreed service available, the Client is generally not entitled to a refund. Subject to any dispute or exception, the Artist becomes eligible for the agreed net payout and Gearsh retains its applicable fees.

8. Completion and deliverables

For live or performance services, completion occurs when the agreed performance or engagement has been delivered, subject to the booking terms. For deliverable-based services, completion occurs when the agreed deliverables have been supplied in accordance with the booking. The completion state may be recorded through Gearsh.

9. Dispute period

The Client has 36 hours after the booking is marked completed to raise a dispute for a live or performance booking. For deliverable-based services, the 36-hour period begins when completion is recorded according to the agreed delivery terms. If a dispute is opened, any applicable held funds are frozen while Gearsh reviews the available evidence.

10. Dispute outcomes

Gearsh may review booking records, the agreement, messages, files, attendance evidence, delivery evidence and other relevant information. Depending on the evidence and the agreed terms, Gearsh may release funds to the Artist, refund the Client, or split the applicable amount. A dispute decision does not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

11. Artist responsibilities

  • Deliver the booked service professionally and as described
  • Attend at the agreed time and location unless otherwise agreed
  • Provide equipment, transport, permits and insurance required for the service where applicable
  • Comply with applicable laws and venue requirements
  • Communicate material changes or problems promptly

12. Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate event, venue, timing and service requirements
  • Provide reasonable access and a safe working environment
  • Make required payments on time
  • Not materially change the agreed service without Artist agreement
  • Communicate cancellations, delays or access problems promptly

13. Independent contractor relationship

The Artist is an independent contractor and is responsible for the Artist's own business, taxes, equipment, insurance, personnel and professional obligations. This agreement does not create employment, partnership, agency or joint-venture status between the Artist and Gearsh.

14. Payout

Once a booking is completed and the applicable 36-hour dispute period has passed, the Artist payout becomes eligible for release through PayFast, subject to no open dispute, refund, chargeback, payment hold or other applicable restriction. PayFast processing and settlement timing may affect the time required for funds to reach the Artist's nominated account.

15. Changes to this agreement

Changes to the service, price, date, deliverables or other material terms should be recorded through Gearsh and agreed by the Client and Artist. Once a booking agreement is signed or otherwise confirmed, material changes should not be treated as agreed unless both parties accept them through the applicable Gearsh process.

16. Relationship with Gearsh Terms

This Booking Service Agreement operates together with the Gearsh Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. If a booking-specific term conflicts with a general platform term, the booking-specific commercial term governs that booking to the extent permitted by law.

17. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, subject to mandatory rights and remedies under applicable law.

18. Contact

Booking and platform questions can be sent to support@thegearsh.com.
