Introduction

Gearsh is a digital marketplace that connects Clients with independent Artists and other creative service providers. Gearsh facilitates discovery, booking requests, agreements, payment processing, dispute handling and payouts, while the underlying creative service is provided by the Artist. By accessing or using the Gearsh application or website, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions.

02

Definitions

"Platform" refers to the Gearsh website, web application, and mobile application. "Artist" refers to an independent individual, group, studio or other creative service provider offering services through the Platform. "Client" refers to any individual or organisation booking services through the Platform. "Booking" refers to a confirmed service agreement between an Artist and a Client. "Artist Subtotal" means the price for the selected Artist service before Gearsh's Client fee. "Gearsh Fees" means the Artist fee and Client fee described below.

06

Financial Terms & Payments

Payments for bookings are processed through Gearsh's payment journey and PayFast where applicable.

  • Artist fee: 4% of the Artist Subtotal.
  • Client fee: 12.6% of the Artist Subtotal, added to the Client's payable amount.
  • Total Gearsh fees: 16.6% of the Artist Subtotal where both fees apply.
  • PayFast processing costs: absorbed by Gearsh and not charged separately to the Client or Artist.
  • Artist deposits: no separate Artist deposit is required under the V1 booking model.

After an Artist accepts a booking request, the Client completes the required payment through Gearsh. Following successful payment, funds are held pending completion of the booking and subject to the applicable cancellation and dispute rules. Once a booking is completed and the applicable dispute period has passed without a dispute or other payment hold, the Artist's payout becomes eligible for release through PayFast. Exact bank receipt timing may depend on payment processing and settlement.

07

Cancellations & Refunds

Unless a different written term is included in the Booking Service Agreement, the following V1 cancellation rules apply:

  • Client cancellation more than 7 days before the booking: 100% of the Artist Subtotal is refunded. Gearsh Fees are retained.
  • Client cancellation 48 hours to 7 days before the booking: 50% of the Artist Subtotal is refunded. Gearsh Fees are retained.
  • Client cancellation less than 48 hours before the booking: 0% of the Artist Subtotal is refunded. Gearsh Fees are retained.
  • Where the Client cancels, the Artist receives R0.
  • Artist cancellation or no-show: the Client receives a full refund, including Gearsh Fees. The Artist receives R0.
  • Client no-show: where the Artist attended or was available as agreed, the Client is not entitled to a refund. The Artist receives the agreed net payout and Gearsh retains its applicable fees.
08

Dispute Resolution

For live and performance bookings, the applicable dispute period is 36 hours after the booking is marked completed. For deliverable-based services, completion is determined according to the Booking Service Agreement and the 36-hour dispute period begins after completion.

If a dispute is opened within the applicable period, the Artist payout is frozen while Gearsh reviews the available booking records, agreement terms, messages and other relevant evidence. Depending on the outcome, Gearsh may release the applicable funds to the Artist, refund the Client, or apply an appropriate split. Unresolved disputes are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa.
